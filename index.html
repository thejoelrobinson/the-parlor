Play against the computer, or link up with a friend peer-to-peer.
Peer-to-peer runs directly between two browsers over WebRTC. You swap two short codes — send them by Bluetooth chat, SMS, or any messenger. (Browsers can't link to each other over Bluetooth itself: Web Bluetooth only reaches BLE gadgets — so WebRTC is the P2P transport, and Bluetooth is just one way to hand over the codes.)
